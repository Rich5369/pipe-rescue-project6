YOUR MISSION

Make a path.
Make it flow.

  1. Rotate the pipes. Click or tap to turn a piece 90° clockwise. Keyboard players can use Tab and Enter or Space.
  2. Connect the openings. Build a continuous route from the source above the grid to the community below it.
  3. Test the water. Press Test Water to check your route. An unsuccessful test costs an attempt and 100 potential points.
  4. Make every turn count. Each rotation costs 10 potential points. A hint fixes one pipe for 75 potential points. Each win earns at least 100 points.
Beat the countdown with three attempts per level. Pause freezes the clock. Restart restores the original arrangement and resets the timer, attempts, and penalties.
